同传 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 13 日

同传是一个中英同声传译 App。我们不收集你的任何数据：没有账号，没有服务器，没有广告，没有第三方统计或分析工具，App 不向开发者或任何第三方发送数据。

1. 麦克风和语音识别

同传需要麦克风来听你和对方说话。声音由 iPhone 上苹果自带的离线语音识别转成文字，只在手机上处理，不上传，也不保存录音。

2. 翻译

翻译使用苹果系统自带的离线翻译，在手机上完成。

3. 模型下载

第一次打开时，iOS 会下载苹果的语音识别模型和中英翻译语言包。这个下载由苹果系统完成，适用苹果隐私政策，App 自己不联网。

4. 不保存记录

屏幕上的原话和译文只在 App 打开时显示，关掉 App 就没了，不会保存到手机或任何地方。

5. 儿童

同传不收集任何人的个人信息，包括 13 岁以下的儿童。

6. 联系我们

如有疑问，请发邮件到 missionary1802@gmail.com。本政策如有变化，会更新在本页面。

Tongchuan Privacy Policy

Effective: 13 November 2026

Tongchuan is a live Chinese-English interpreter. We collect no data at all: no account, no server, no ads, no analytics or tracking tools. The app sends nothing to the developer or any third party.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is used to hear you and the other person. Speech is turned into text by Apple's on-device speech recognition, only on your iPhone. Audio is never uploaded or saved.

Translation

Translation uses Apple's built-in on-device translation.

Model downloads

On first launch, iOS downloads Apple's speech models and Chinese-English translation languages. That download is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy; the app itself makes no network requests.

Nothing saved

Transcripts and translations are shown only while the app is open and are gone when you close it.

Children

Tongchuan collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Email missionary1802@gmail.com. Any changes will be posted on this page.
